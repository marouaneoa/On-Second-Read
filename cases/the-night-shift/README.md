# The night shift

Leila is applying to supervise evening maintenance at a theatre. An evening supervisor sometimes leaves before the morning crew arrives, so a repair may cross two shifts. The panel asks how Leila handled a faulty light above the stage. The file includes a message written at midnight and a record completed by the incoming technician.

## What the panel agreed to assess

A safe handover requires the next worker to know which equipment is unsafe and who will take responsibility. Look for what the outgoing worker communicated and what the incoming worker understood.

## Read the file

**p1 · Outgoing shift message**

I placed a red tag on lamp four and switched its circuit off. My message to the morning crew said to leave that lamp isolated until someone tested the cable. I sent the message to the shared shift phone at midnight. I left the line for the receiving technician’s name blank because nobody had arrived.

**p2 · Morning receipt**

At six I read Leila’s message on the shift phone. I called her and repeated the lamp number and the instruction to leave its power off. She confirmed both details. I entered my name as the person responsible for testing the cable and told the stage manager to use lamp five instead.

## Listen to the reviewers

**Reviewer A · cites p1**

From the outgoing message I know Leila identified the hazard. The empty name line leaves me wondering whether anybody actually took over. I would ask for a receipt before giving full credit for the handover.

**Reviewer B · cites p2**

The morning receipt closes that gap for me. The technician repeated the instruction to Leila and signed for the cable test. I can identify the person who accepted responsibility and the equipment they discussed.

Before opening the answer, name the standard each reviewer used and point to the words each one relied on.

### How to read the evidence

Put the two records in time order. At midnight the blank line genuinely leaves a question. At six the named technician answers it. The reviewers are using the same safety requirement at different points along the paper trail. An unanswered question in the earlier record is compatible with a completed handover in the later one.
