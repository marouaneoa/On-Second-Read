What counts as evidence?

Two people can read the same application and come away with different judgments. Your job is to follow each reviewer back to the words they relied on.

Reader’s guide

How to read a case

  1. Find the promise. Read the published rubric. It tells you what the panel agreed to assess. Each standard, such as clear instructions, is a criterion.
  2. Trace the judgment. Read both reviews. Match each claim to a sentence in the dossier. Ask whether the reviewers used different evidence or expected different things.
  3. Make your call. Choose a pattern and the detail that supports it. Open the reveal to compare your reading with the documented links. Then change one link in the Evidence Lab and see what follows.
